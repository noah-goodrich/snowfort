# 20260725-voice-era-no-global-lock

## decision

Do not lock the skill to a single voice era globally. Instead, combine Long Game era mechanics with tooling-launch era structure specifically for the WAF technical series.

## context

The 2026-05-23 recommendations raised whether to lock the skill to a specific era for consistency. Two eras were relevant: Long Game (published, validated prose mechanics) and tooling-launch ('Origin Story' structural spine).

## reasoning

A global lock is too coarse — different series types need different structural patterns even if prose mechanics stay consistent. The WAF series needs the tooling-launch 'Origin Story' spine because snowfort is the subject, but Long Game mechanics (short sentences, contractions, weight-bearing landings, 'Your move' CTA, italic coda) are the proven voice. Combining them is more accurate than forcing a single era.

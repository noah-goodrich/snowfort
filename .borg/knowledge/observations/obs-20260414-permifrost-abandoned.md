# obs-20260414-permifrost-abandoned

## content

Permifrost, a commonly referenced Snowflake RBAC/IaC tool, was archived in 2024 and is effectively abandoned. It should not be recommended or adopted for new Snowflake projects.

## resolution

Use DCM Projects (native Snowflake IaC, Preview as of March 2026) or raw SQL scripts. Terraform remains an option at larger scale.
